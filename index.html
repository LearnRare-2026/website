Research, reimagined

Rare-disease research often involves fragmented evidence, limited datasets and complex questions. LearnRare investigates focused questions using computational analysis and publicly available evidence. Each project is deliberately concise, reproducible and transparent — turning a focused research question into a complete investigation and an accessible scientific output.

Focused

Small, clearly defined questions rather than sprawling investigations.

Rigorous

Evidence-driven computational analysis with explicit, checked assumptions.

Accessible

Findings communicated visually and openly, without unnecessary jargon.

What makes it different

Every project is held to a minimum research standard: a focused question, a genuine gap, appropriate evidence, a defensible method and a meaningful potential contribution.

High-frequency research

Many focused investigations rather than a small number of large projects.

Rare-disease focus

A coherent scientific identity that carries across every project.

AI-assisted methodology

AI is used transparently for appropriate tasks such as literature discovery and coding, while research design and conclusions stay human-led.

Short-form reporting

Visual-first communication

About LearnRare

LearnRare exists because rare-disease research is uniquely challenged by underrepresentation of small patient size diseases, low access of data and lack of awareness in both the research community and public space. Consequentally, research efforts for rare diseases are often slow, meaning that less innovations to happening to impact those with these conditions, resulting in systemic health inequities that need to be addressed.

Our initiative serves to fulfill three main objectives

Firstly, we utilise the incredible computational power of AI innovations to drive investigations that produce new insight more efficiently whilst adhereing to a high scientific standard.

Secondly, we aim to improve accessibility of our findings to appeal broader audiences, such as students or budding health professionals, those living with rare diseases or their family, and up to established researchers and medical professionals, compared to traditional academic literature. We do this by reporting our studies using short, concise reports with engaging visuals rather than long conventional paper - designed to be understood, not just published.

Thirdly, our long-term goal is to produce a growing, interconnected body of independent research, where many small questions are answered with evidence-based inquiries and shared with the public, promoting awareness to the challenges and inequities faced by rare diseases.

AI-driven, accessible and raising awareness - we are motivated to making a real impact in rare disease research, one step at a time.
